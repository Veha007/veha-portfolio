Overview

A friend runs a small book business and sold mainly through Facebook and Telegram. She had a strong community, but customers struggled to browse her catalog, and she spent hours answering the same questions about stock, prices and payment.

I designed a dedicated e-commerce website to give the business one professional, easy-to-use home. Customers can discover books and check out smoothly, while the sales process runs itself. Key decisions: a fully responsive layout, light and dark themes, and popular Cambodian payment methods to build trust with local buyers.

The designs shown are a recreation of the original concept, rebranded with my own logo for portfolio purposes.

Problem & solution
01 · Problem

Selling through chat

Social media sales felt unprofessional. Browsing was messy, checkout was uncertain, and the seller was stuck doing repetitive manual work.

02 · UX

A clear, linear journey

An organised catalog, dedicated product pages and a simple, secure one-page checkout replace the chaotic chat flow.

03 · UI

A trustworthy brand

A clean, modern UI with clear hierarchy and consistent components. Light and dark modes improve comfort and accessibility.

Scope of work
  • Fully responsive designA consistent experience on desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • E-commerce componentsCatalog grid, promo banners, search and a multi-option payment module.
  • Dual-theme systemComplete, accessible UI in both light and dark mode.
  • Localized checkoutABA Bank, Wing, ACLEDA, Bakong and card payments for the local market.
