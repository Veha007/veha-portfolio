Frame 05Process

How I work

The same five steps on every project, so the team always knows what comes next.

  1. 01

    Research

    Talk to users and stakeholders, and map the real workflow.

  2. 02

    Wireframe

    Low-fidelity flows to test ideas before polishing.

  3. 03

    Design

    High-fidelity UI for web, tablet and mobile.

  4. 04

    Prototype

    Animated prototypes in Figma and Lottie to prove the details.

  5. 05

    Hand-off

    Design system components and specs developers can build from.
